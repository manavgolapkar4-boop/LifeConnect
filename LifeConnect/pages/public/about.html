About LifeConnect

Connecting donors. Supporting lives.

Our mission

LifeConnect exists to close the gap between someone who needs blood and someone willing to give it. Every year, avoidable delays in finding a compatible, nearby donor put patients at risk — we're building the search and matching layer that should already exist.

This project is a working prototype built for a college semester project, demonstrating how such a platform could be structured end-to-end.

Why blood donation matters

A single donation can support multiple patients — from accident victims to those undergoing surgery or long-term treatment. Blood cannot be manufactured; it only comes from donors, and it's needed continuously, not just during crises.

  • Whole blood donations can typically be given roughly every 3 months.
  • Rare blood groups are harder to find nearby, which is where location-based search helps most.
  • Hospitals depend on a steady, predictable supply — not just emergency spikes.
